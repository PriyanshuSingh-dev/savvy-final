Join SFA – Build Your Future in Finance, Valuation & Technology

At SFA, we are a growing startup that values innovation, knowledge, and professional growth. Whether you're looking for an internship, training, or a full-time career, we offer a dynamic and friendly work environment where you can enhance your skills and gain hands-on experience in finance, valuation, engineering, and technology.

Opportunities at SFA

Finance & Valuation: Work on financial analysis, audits, and business valuation projects. Open to BBA, MBA, Chartered Accountants (CAs), and Polytechnic students.
Engineering & Planning: Contribute to designing, structuring, and valuation-related projects. Preferred branches include B.Tech, M.Tech in Civil, Mechanical Engineering, computer science, Architecture, and Polytechnic students.
Technology & Development:Help build and manage our backend systems, website, and app. Open to students from Computer Science (CS) and IT backgrounds.


Who Can Apply?

Students and graduates from Polytechnic, BBA, MBA, B.Tech, M.Tech, Civil, Mechanical, Architecture, Chartered Accountancy (CA), and CS streams looking for a learning-oriented career in finance, valuation, or technology.

Internship Program

Our internship program is designed for students who want to gain real-world experience in finance, valuation, engineering, and technology. We offer: Short-term and long-term internships Practical exposure to live projects Mentorship from industry experts Certificate of completion Opportunity for a pre-placement offer based on performance

Training Program

Our training program is ideal for fresh graduates or individuals looking to upskill in specific areas. We provide: Guidance from experienced professionals Project-based learning with case studies Certification upon successful completion Potential job opportunities at SFA after training

Full-Time Job Opportunities

For professionals and skilled graduates, we offer full-time roles across different domains. Competitive salaries and growth opportunities Professional work environment with industry experts Performance-based promotions and career advancement
