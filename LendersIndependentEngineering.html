APPROVED PROJECT FINANCE (APF)

Independent Oversight, Trusted Reporting :-

At SFA LLP, we provide professional Lender’s Independent Engineer (LIE) services that offer unbiased technical oversight for projects financed by banks and financial institutions. With a multidisciplinary team of engineers, valuation experts, and project managers, SFA LLP delivers detailed technical reviews, risk assessments, and periodic progress reports. These help lenders make informed disbursement decisions while minimizing exposure to potential construction or financial risks.

From pre-sanction due diligence to post-disbursement monitoring, we ensure that each project adheres to both regulatory standards and lender expectations. Our LIE reports are transparent, structured, and defensible, ensuring accountability and confidence throughout the funding lifecycle.
